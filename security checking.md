
Security Checklist

- [x] Certifications and compliance — Logos and names of security certifications held e.g. SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA
  - 💡 Certification logos should be paired with an explanation of what they cover
- [x] Data encryption — A plain-language explanation of how data is encrypted at rest and in transit
- [x] Data residency — Where customer data is stored and processed, with any regional options available
- [x] Access controls — Who within the company can access customer data, under what conditions, and what controls are in place to limit that access
- [x] Vulnerability disclosure — How security issues are reported and handled e.g. a dedicated email address, a bug bounty programme, or a responsible disclosure policy
- [x] Incident history — A record of past security incidents that were disclosed, with dates and resolution summaries for transparency
- [x] Penetration testing — Details of third-party security audits or penetration tests, with the most recent date and ability to request a report for further details
